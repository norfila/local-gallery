Gallery's copy is generated by scripts/build_countries.py: rings are simplified
(Douglas-Peucker, 0.006 degrees), coordinates are rounded to 3 decimals, small
islands keep all points, and unused attributes are stripped.
Country codes come from ISO_A2_EH, with ADM0_A3 used when no ISO code is assigned.
Names come from NAME_EN; standard ISO country names are used at runtime when
available. Natural Earth uses de facto boundaries. Points outside the land
polygons use the nearest country within 25 km (beaches, harbours, islets);
open-sea points further away stay unmatched.
